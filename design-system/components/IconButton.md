# IconButton

Icon-only buttons: white line icons, in two forms, always with an `aria-label`.

- **On image** (44px circle, `overlay` fill, 20–22px icon): back, save, share and mute on top of photos and video.
- **Action rail** (56px wide, 30px icon with a 12px 700 label or count underneath): the save, share and more column on the right of a swipe card. A saved bookmark fills `brand`.
- **Sheet target** (58px circle, `surface-300` fill, 24px icon, 13px label under): share options.

Keep the tap area at least `touch-min` even when the icon is smaller.
