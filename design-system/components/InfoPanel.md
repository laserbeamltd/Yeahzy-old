# InfoPanel

A dark rounded panel of key facts, used for the date and venue on the event detail and for line-ups.

- `surface-100` fill, `radius-lg`, rows padded 14px 16px and split by 1px `line-100`–`#242424` hairlines.
- Each row: a 22px white line icon, a `body-strong` title with a `meta` line under it in `text-secondary`, and an optional outline Button on the right ("Add", "Directions").
- Keep it to two or three rows; longer lists belong on their own screen.
