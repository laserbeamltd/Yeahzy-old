# Button

Pill-shaped buttons in three weights: primary yellow, secondary dark and small outline.

- **Primary** (`brand` fill, `on-brand` text, `button` style, `button-height`, `radius-pill`): one per screen, usually full width and pinned `space-28` from the bottom. On swipe cards it shrinks to 46px high with `card-body` 700 text ("Get tickets · £12").
- **Secondary** (`surface-200` fill, `text-primary`): alternatives that sit under a primary, e.g. "Continue with Apple".
- **Outline** (transparent, 1px `line-300` border, 36px high, 13px 700): inline actions inside panels ("Add", "Directions").

Don't put two primary buttons on one screen, and don't use yellow for destructive actions.
