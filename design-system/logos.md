# Logos

The official Yeahzy logo: the wordmark "Yeahzy" in Bricolage Grotesque 800 at 96 optical size, -0.055em tracking, outlined to vector paths so it looks the same everywhere without the font.

- `yeahzy-logo-black.svg`: ink `#0a0a0a` (`on-brand`). Use on the yellow splash and any `brand` background.
- `yeahzy-logo-yellow.svg`: `#ffe500` (`brand`). The default on `ink` and dark photography.
- `yeahzy-logo-white.svg`: `#ffffff` (`text-primary`). On busy images or where yellow is already doing a job on screen.

Clear space: at least the height of the lowercase "e" on every side. Minimum width: 80px on screen. Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or retype it.
